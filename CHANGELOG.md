# Changelog

All notable changes to Afterlife Lease are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).
The version number has one source, `package.json` → `"version"` (see docs/SHIP_CHECKLIST.md §1).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **The project is open.** The code and script-generated art are under the MIT licence; the files it does not cover are listed in `CREDITS.md`. Contributions come by fork and pull request (`CONTRIBUTING.md`), with a code of conduct.
- `docs/CORE_CONCEPT.md`: the guide for builders. Your household at your own pace; a shared town where nobody is attacked; missions you queue for; a protected main home and defended extra homes; hordes, crews and blocks; code builders and in-game builders. Seven fixed rules.
- `src/shared/homeDefense.ts`: the schedule rules for attacks on extra homes (weekly window, grace period, passes, the fighting-mode switch, changing the main home) as tested rules. Nothing in the game calls them yet.
- The fighting mode is in or out: the switch locks for 48 hours after a change and after every attack made, and a switch-off during an open window takes effect when the window closes. Switching off is how a household goes on vacation, so the separate vacation shield is gone.
- `ROADMAP.md`: where the game is going (single-player next steps, Frontier mode and Eviction Night, online play) and the rules each area must respect.
- README screenshots, regenerated by `node scripts/capture-screenshots.mjs`.
- A workflow that publishes the playable web build to GitHub Pages from `main` (needs Pages switched on in the repository settings).
- `docs/community/ASSETS.md` §6: tools contributors can use for art (Blender scripts, ComfyUI, other generators) and the provenance rule for AI-made art.
- Animals stay outdoors: wild critters never walk, perch or fly through a room below roof height, and strays cannot step from outside into a room (only an adopted pet lives indoors).
- W-094 source-bound web/Windows build receipts and artifact verification, with executed unsigned NSIS packaging and installer tamper/restore evidence. Signing, installed upgrade/rollback and staged rollout remain separate release gates.
- The render showcase now applies gameplay sofa fitting and keeps its scripted walkers off occupied tiles, with regression checks for both.
- Premium clipping audit: all five bodies at high/low detail, standing plate/book fallbacks, carried movement and held-item stage transitions; exact capped baseline, missing/separated prop controls and a separate 60 Hz pickup-rise guard. See `docs/desktop-reports/sol-premium-clip-coverage.md` for measured existing defects and verification.
- New pet adoptions stop at three; existing four-pet saves retain every animal and remain fully careable. The schema-3 migration gives pre-cosmetic pets their normal look without classifying a valid older save as damaged. Pet errands cancel when their zombie leaves home, and home pets use only home-region positions when choosing where to move.
- Active neighbouring food ages while visited, fires advance and affect only local zombies, and stereo loops stop on freeze and resume on return. Household bills, mail and jobs retain their home boundaries.
- Walkable-world integration reuses the validated varied-start generator and gives its seven neighbour slots the same deterministic templates in simulation and rendering; streaming builds furnished homes and retains modified snapshots. Vacant lots stay empty, and shell origins and doors follow actual lot bounds. Approved schema 3 migrates both published jobs-v2 and internal world-v2 saves; jobs retain progress while Away and resume at home. Household bills, mail, quests and local activity discovery respect region boundaries. See `docs/desktop-reports/sol-world-conflict-map.md` for integration evidence.
- W-025-S11 reusable world navigation controls, accessible destination list and readable sign dialog; tap-only gesture adapter, route refusal feedback, selection-race guards, 44 px targets and phone scrolling. Native 400% zoom exposed a hidden Close control; a definite viewport height cap fixes it, with the original-rule negative control and 100/200/400% retest retained. S5 is integrated; production map/sign entry points continue in the approved S13 follow-up. Consolidated input fixes enable actual region-aware mouse/touch round trips, prevent orders from mixed HUD/world gestures or changed selection, and return away members to the explicit home region; Recentre targets the selected member.
- W-025 S4: stream nearby regions and walker destinations, freeze/thaw snapshots, and apply deterministic catch-up; public-yard routing and private-interior refusal are checked across the streaming lifecycle.
- W-025 S5: draw connected-world regions, warm shells, streets and unfinished boundaries; follow/recentre frames the selected zombie clear of HUD panels across phone orientations.
- Roofs follow-up (docs/contract-requests/roofs-views.md rules 1-4 and 6): over the shoulder inside a building the ceiling of your room is shown (not faded out); every roof on the lot is hidden from above (overview and shoulder, whoever is where: all lot buildings are the household home), and in Build and Buy; first person keeps roofs; the roof layer is level-aware by design (a building's `levels`, roof on the top level, ceilings per level; one level today). Fades are carried by tile overlap, so a wall that splits a room or renumbered room ids no longer snap a roof or swap a ceiling's opacity. Real-Chrome evidence in docs/desktop-reports/evidence/roofs-views/.
- W-013 pet rules from the owner's living-things direction (docs/contract-requests/living-things.md, pets part): a household holds at most 3 pets and 2 of one species (new adoptions only: an older save keeps what it has), a looked-after pet gives the household company (full care +4 mood, half care +2, neglect none; a Guard pet holds the Repo Ghoul off twice at full care, once at half care), and seeded, capped encounters from one data table: a dog or cat may chase off a stray dog or cat it sees (about 25 % per sighting, once a day per pet, never when its care is low; the stray comes back in 2 to 4 days), a cat after a songbird or pigeon or a dog after a squirrel gives a comic chase (the critter always gets away, at most twice a day), and a well-cared pet occasionally brings a small find ($3 to $12, once a day). Shared rule table in `src/shared/livingThings.ts` for plants and other living things.
- Home build, steps 3 and 4 (docs/contract-requests/home-build-system.md): the Hearth, Wardrobe Coffin, Crypt Vault, Bone Workbench and Bone Gate (five new objects with models in their own Windows kit, `tools/blender/homeservices`, pinned in `toolchains.json`). Click the Hearth, Wardrobe, Embalming Station or Crypt Vault for its screen: Hearth upgrades bought with Rot Essence (earned daily while the Hearth stands and the utilities are on), the Wardrobe's saved builds (4 per zombie, 10 game-minute swap cooldown), the Embalming Station's once-a-day respec, the vault's seven materials. Saving a build and the respec stay off until zombie builds are in the game (one flag, `BUILD_DATA_READY`); the Workbench and Bone gate are placed furniture only until crafting and the town map exist; the vault is read-only until salvage exists.
- Home life bonuses: furniture sets (2/4/6 pieces) and Hearth wards make Rest faster while asleep, add Fun, skill gain and Freshness asleep, and start the day with a feeling (Well Interred, Cosy Parlour, Well Fed). They count in a world made with Snap-point building or once the Hearth is placed (D-086), switch off with the utilities, and never touch a fight stat. The household panel lists the bonuses in force.
- No Wardrobe swap or Embalming respec during a fire, Fight Fire, a Repo Ghoul at the door, a get-together, or while the `home.buildLock` world flag is set (for the future combat and raid systems). The saved-build payload has a validator hook for the zombie build data.
- Row 9 (installed upgrade and rollback with saves kept): `tests/desktop/row9-upgrade-rollback.mjs` and `docs/desktop-reports/opus2-row9-upgrade-rollback.md`; the appearance hash of every zombie is unchanged across upgrade, rollback and re-upgrade (24 of 24 checks).
- Two boot-enabled Spark1 CI services with isolated workspaces, their own shared 16 GiB / 6 CPU / no-swap ceiling, and locked evidence archival; Sol's manual lane remains separate.
- Two Spark2 CI services with separate workspaces, a shared 16 GiB / 6 CPU ceiling, serialized evidence archival and tested activation rollback.
- W-013: **undead pet looks** (stitched, glowing-eyed cat, dog, raccoon and crow) as a Grave Gold store unlock per species, cosmetic only. A pet has a Look (Normal / Undead) in the Household panel's Pets section; the Store's Bodies tab lists the four unlocks as "coming soon" and neither Bones nor Grave Gold can buy them yet (`PET_VARIANTS_LIVE`), so the Undead look stays locked and a click is refused with a message. Unlike the premium cast bodies they are not free in the meantime. The look is saved with the pet.
- W-013 / W-075: **pets and animals are in the game.** Strays (a cat, dog, raccoon or crow) turn up day by day: street strays wander the sidewalk, graveyard strays the back of the lot, and a chased one bolts along the sidewalk. Click one with a zombie selected for Offer Scraps, Befriend, Pet, Adopt (at trust 70) or Chase; an adopted pet lives around the household, walks to a spot near its zombies each quarter hour, sleeps at night, and eats, plays or sits when cared for (Feed / Play / Cuddle from its click menu or the new Pets section of the Household panel). Ambient wildlife (songbirds, pigeons, squirrels, bats, frogs) is drawn too. Positions, facing and animation are deterministic sim state and are saved. A neglected pet runs away and returns as a stray; none ever dies. Models load only when an animal is in view and are released with the renderer. Review fixes: a chase has a 2 h cool-down (no free fun), animals never get spoiled food, an old save starts with a street and a graveyard stray, and the chase test can fail (a trusted stray loses exactly 10 trust and stays). Pet names always come from the preset pools, so a child profile never types one. A zombie walks to the animal before a menu action happens (a saved, cancellable "Go Here" walk), and a looked-after Guard pet runs to the gate, barks (a synthesized bark sound, no samples) and holds the Repo Ghoul off for 12 game hours, twice per debt.

### Fixed
- World boundary save/load checks run each of the 34 save points as its own test, preserving every replay assertion while avoiding the aggregate 60-second timeout on shared runners.
- Home build/buy and home services use the household's region. Neighbour furniture cannot block home redo, count toward home caps/sets/pay, or be sold or removed with a home wall. Building relocates home actors only; station commands refuse away members, and foreign fires leave home-service eligibility unchanged.
- Renderer warm-up stats now record cumulative material precompile calls and elapsed milliseconds; the long-soak samples and receipt report these counters.
- Render warm-up revisions now advance for any sanitized zombie appearance change, including same-body material variants; an unchanged appearance does not trigger redundant compilation. Regression: `tests/unit/characters/rig.test.ts`.
- D-085 (rating stays E10+): the premium bodies were checked against the content rules and three were repainted at the source: The Sexton's torn cheeks (teeth and jaw showing) are closed, scarred skin; The Drowned's empty eye socket is a clouded eye like the other; Lowside's raw forearm gash and temple laceration are softened to skin. The Night Shift and Bed Nine needed nothing. Rebuilt with `build_premium.py --only sexton,fisherman,lowside`; new thumbnails; the repaint is recorded in `tools/blender/cast/premium/PROVENANCE.md` (step 5) with the before and after texture SHA-256.
- Premium zombie bodies now require their profile entitlement in Create-a-Zombie and when opening a save; kit bodies stay free and child profiles see no premium price.
- W-152: **picked-up items no longer pass through the leg.** In the shared `pickup` clip the item was shown in the hand from the first frame, while the arm still swung down past the thigh: 30–150 mm into the leg on every body (Classic, cast and premium). An item that arrives with the pickup (fridge, shelf, floor) now appears when the hand has reached it (0.45 s of the 1.2 s clip); an item already carried into it (Throw Away) stays in the hand throughout. Clip audit `pen:item-body` on pickup: 7 FAILs to 0, nothing else changed. The faults that need a clip change (shins crossing, hand into the floor, the rise along the legs) are measured in `docs/desktop-reports/opus2-pickup.md` for the lead.
- W-152 (premium bodies, open item 1): the seating test reported the premium bodies' toes inside the Haunted Desktop PC's knee space, which is empty. The open sheet ghost on the monitor made the test's solid grid fill the whole column below it down to the floor. The grid now counts each mesh separately, and an open shell is solid only down to its own bottom. The desk exemption is removed: every seat passes for the Classic, the cast kit and all five premium bodies, and a new check fails with the old grid (`docs/desktop-reports/opus2-desk-knee-space.md`). Test-only; no furniture or runtime change.
- Recurring household jobs now stop queued or active work when their daily window closes, including overnight windows, and resume in the next window.
- W-134 (D-074): crash recovery was dropped, and up to all play since the last save lost, when the page's main thread was busy for more than 2 s while the game waited for the journal lock on load (asset decoding, shader compiles, slow or software-rendered frames). The wait was a wall-clock timeout, so the game's own stall read as "another window is playing this world". The lock wait and the boot-time settings / tutorial reads now use `withResponsiveTimeout` (budget spent only while the thread is responsive); a lock that never answers is logged instead of silent. Reproduced in real Chromium on the batch-1106 build with `node tests/e2e/journal.mjs --stall-ms=3000` (27 s of play lost), fixed build passes at 3 s and 15 s stalls.
- W-134: `tests/e2e/journal.mjs` killed the tab 0 ms after the last action when the machine was loaded and then reported the journal as lossy; the kill now always comes 1.5 s after the last action.

## [1.0.0] — Unreleased

First ship build: Windows desktop (Electron, NSIS x64 per-user installer) and web (multi-file `dist/` and the
single-file `dist-single/`).

### Added

- **Gameplay**
  - Life-sim loop for a household of zombies in Wormwood Heights.
  - Six needs (Appetite, Rest, Freshness, Company, Amusement, Odor Control) and the decay economy: Freshness never
    fully resets, plus limb loss.
  - Moods and moodlets, 18 traits (three per zombie), skills 0–10, and five career tracks × five
    levels with a carpool hearse and work-choice events.
  - 24 seeded townies, walk-bys, drop-ins, invitations, parties, romance, five aspirations with a Reward Store,
    random life events, bills and power.
  - Utility-AI autonomy (full / limited / off).
- **Create-a-Zombie**: 1–4 members, look, traits, aspiration, voice, randomize.
- **Build & Buy**: walls, room tool, doors and windows, floors, wall paint, 81 object and environment models;
  exact-refund undo/redo within a session. Two lots: "Fixer-Upper on Mildew Lane" and "Empty Plot".
- **Time**: pause, 1×, 2×, 3× and ultra speed (when every household zombie is asleep or at work). Day/night cycle and
  full moon.
- **Rendering**: Three.js orbit camera with 45° rotation steps, cutaway wall modes, static batching, FX, thumbnails,
  quality levels.
- **Audio**: fully procedural (WebAudio): SFX, the "Groanish" zombie voice, an adaptive score and ambience.
- **Saves**: SHA-256-verified envelopes, verify-before-write, atomic writes, automatic backup of the previous good
  save, schema migrations, `.alsave` export/import, autosave and save-on-quit. Electron file store,
  IndexedDB / localStorage / in-memory fallbacks.
- **Desktop**: sandboxed renderer on the `app://game/` protocol with a strict CSP, a single-instance lock, crash/hang
  recovery, window placement memory, a rotating log, and Electron fuses.
- **Tooling**
  - `scripts/build-windows.ps1` release pipeline, single-file web build, asset SHA-256 verification.
  - GitHub Actions CI (Windows + Ubuntu): typecheck, tests, builds, asset checks, Windows installer artifact,
    optional e2e.
- **Docs**: README, ARCHITECTURE, TESTING, SHIP_CHECKLIST, PLATFORM, IP_NOTES, THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.
