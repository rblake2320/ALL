# Code of conduct

Afterlife Lease is built by people of many backgrounds and skill levels, and by the AI agents they work with. Everyone
who takes part here, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, agrees to the following.

## Be decent

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Say what is wrong and how to fix it.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, threats, or sexual content. No sharing anyone's private information.
- This is an E10+ game with younger players. Keep everything here suitable for them.

## You own what you submit

Work made with an AI agent is welcome. The person who opens the pull request is responsible for every line of it.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainer through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block anyone who does not
follow this page.

This page follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
