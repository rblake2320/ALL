No fonts, images or audio samples from third parties are shipped. All audio is synthesised at runtime
(`src/audio/`). The 3D models are generated by our own Blender scripts (§3), with three exceptions that are **not**
procedural and are listed here so nobody reads this file as saying otherwise. This list was made from the build
records and a text search of the shipped files (2026-11-12); a file that carries no marker cannot be found by a
search, so a full provenance sweep of `public/assets/` is still owed before a public release:

* **The five premium character bodies** (`public/assets/models/characters/premium/*.glb`, W-152) were made with
  generative AI services: concept images through Meshy's text-to-image tool (image model `gpt-image-2-5-sunburst`,
  as Meshy names it), 3D models and their textures with Tripo
  (model `v3.1-20260211`), rigged with Tripo's biped rig (Mixamo-style bone names). Our scripts
  (`tools/blender/cast/premium/`) then re-pose, re-skin to the game's 22-bone rig, decimate, re-bake and export
  them; the animations are the game's own. The owner confirmed on 2026-11-19 that these were made on paid plans, which by
  those services' published terms give the account holder the rights to the output (see `CREDITS.md`). **Counsel
  has not reviewed those terms (W-095).** Store pages that
  ask about AI-generated content must be answered accordingly.
* **The 25 cast bodies** (`public/assets/models/characters/cast/*.glb`) are imported from the owner's own earlier
  game, Zombie Smash 2 (decision D-004), and processed by `tools/blender/cast/`. They are the owner's assets, not
  third-party ones, and not procedural.
* **The Rot Row jukebox** (`public/assets/models/levels/rotrow/jukebox_ghoul.glb`, W-053) is adapted from a Meshy
  text-to-3D model (the file's own copyright field says so). The same open counsel item applies.

Not shipped, for completeness: the loading-screen paintings in `art/ui/loading/` were made with an image generator
(`art/ui/loading.json`); they are source art in the repository and are in no build today. Much of the code and
documentation was written with AI coding assistants under the owner's direction (the commit history names them).

### Note on Blender / `bpy` (GPL)

The 3D models in `public/assets/models/` (other than the premium and cast character bodies named in §1) are **generated** by our own Python scripts (`tools/blender/objects/**`,
`tools/blender/characters/**`, `tools/blender/common/**`), run through headless Blender (`bpy`).

* Blender's GPL covers Blender itself. The Blender Foundation's license FAQ states that what you make with Blender
  (models, renders, exported files) is yours, and the GPL does not extend to that output. Our generated GLB files
  are therefore ours, under the project license.
* **`bpy` is not shipped** with the game, its installer or its web build, and it is not linked into any shipped
  code. It runs only as a build tool.
* The generator scripts import `bpy`, and they stay in this private repository. If those scripts are ever
  distributed on their own, check the GPL implications for that distribution first. This does not affect the game.
