# Design pillars — Sims × Minecraft × Fortnite-style building (owner direction 2026-11-05)

Look: **smooth, modern, stylised 3D — never blocky/voxel.** Rating E10+. Original IP only (mechanics are fair game;
names, characters, art, trade dress are not).

## A. The Sims pillar — a life worth managing
Needs, moods, traits, skills, careers, relationships, aspirations, build/buy, Create-a-Zombie (all exist), plus:
- **Look Alive** (signature system): zombies want to *pass as living*. A hidden **Passing** score (0–100) from makeup,
  wigs, cologne/perfume, clothes condition, posture training, freshness and limb state. Mirror interactions: *Practise
  Smile*, *Apply Makeup*, *Fix Wig*, *Rehearse Small Talk* (skill: **Charm**). High Passing unlocks living-world venues
  and events (job interviews, dates with "warm" townies, the Mausoleum Hills country club); a slip (limb falls off,
  groan in public) drops Passing with comedic consequences. Store: premium makeup kits, wigs, outfits (cosmetic only).
- **Mirror Cam / Photo mode** (later): mirror "selfie" view, photo booth, in-game camera, gallery + share cards.
- **Life arc without ageing:** **Rot Stages** (Fresh → Seasoned → Vintage → Legendary) by days survived; household
  growth by *turning* willing townies (recruit), weddings, house guests, rivals, family trees.
- Hobbies & collections, seasons + weather + holidays (Halloween = Christmas for zombies), parties, lot traits.

## B. Minecraft pillar — gather, craft, dig, make the world yours (smooth, not blocks)
- **Salvage materials** from free sources (graveyard digging, dumpsters, junk piles, demolishing objects): Scrap, Bone,
  Cloth, Wood, Glass, Wire, Ectoplasm (rare). Stored in a household **Stash**.
- **Crafting stations:** Workbench, Sewing Table (exists), Tinker Bench, Embalming Station (exists) → craft furniture,
  decor, upgrades, vehicle parts, makeup, pet gear. Recipes unlocked by skill/quests/store. Crafted items = free path
  to things others buy (supports the "out of money" play style).
- **Dig layer** = L6 The Underneath: dig basements, crypts, tunnels under any lot; find materials and secrets.
- **The world grows** = L7 The Necropolis: blueprints, expanding town, player-made content.

## C. Fortnite-style building — fast, fun construction
- **Quick Build mode** alongside classic Build: snap-together modular pieces (wall, floor, stairs, ramp, roof, arch,
  half-wall, platform, balcony, pillar) placed with one click on a smooth grid, rotate with one key, **edit** a placed
  piece (cut a window/door/arch hole in a wall, change a roof shape) via a 3×3 edit grid; drag-to-build runs; instant
  undo. Pieces have materials (wood → stone → metal tiers) and cost $ or crafted materials. Multi-storey + decks.
- Same sim rules (rooms, environment, pathing) apply to Quick Build pieces — one building system, two input styles.

## D. Vehicles — buy, build, enhance
- **Bike → Moped → Car (beater sedan) → Hearse → Monster hearse/premium rides.** Buy with $ (household), craft from
  salvage (Minecraft path), or premium skins (GG). Parked on the lot (driveway/garage objects).
- **Garage / Tinker Bench upgrades:** paint & decals, wheels, engine (speed), horn sounds, lights, seats (capacity),
  trunk (cargo for salvage), "Look Alive" upgrades (tinted windows hide your face → Passing at destinations).
- **Gameplay:** travel between lots/levels faster than walking or carpool (W-025 travel time scales by vehicle),
  carpool friends, deliver for a side gig (Delivery career/odd jobs), breakdowns + repair (Handiness/Tinkering skill),
  fuel = "Embalming fluid" cheap consumable. Vehicles visible on streets; townies drive too.
- Store tie-ins: vehicle skins, premium rides, sponsor house brands (Volt-Hearse EV).

## E. Make-money paths (ties to docs/MONETIZATION.md)
Free: jobs, odd jobs, selling crafted items & grave goods, quests (Bones). Paid: GG cosmetics, bundles, vehicles skins,
season pass, expansions (new levels/careers). Never sell power that skips the core loop.
