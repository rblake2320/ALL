# W-077 Furniture choice & tiers, "power" furniture, contractors — owner direction (2026-11-05)

## 1. Real choice in every category (fix the "coffin vs bed" confusion)
Beds already exist as separate options (Budget Burial Plot $80, Pine Box Coffin $300, Chill-Rest Refrigerated Coffin
$1,400, Cryo Sarcophagus $4,800, **Suspiciously Normal Bed $500**); the Coffin-Lid Coffee Table is a *table*, not a
bed. The store must make this obvious: **Beds** shows sub-filters *Coffins · Beds · Plots*, every item card states its
category and function, and search finds "bed" for both. Rule for every category: at least one **regular** (human-style)
option and one **zombie** option, so players decorate the way they like.

## 2. Tiers per furniture type: Basic → Fancy
Each category gets a ladder (target 4–6 items): **Junk** (free/salvage) → **Basic** → **Nice** → **Fancy** →
**Luxury**, plus **Signature** pieces (earn-only/collection). Higher tiers raise comfort/quality/environment score (existing
stats) and look better; prices follow MONETIZATION item bands. Example — fridges: Rusty Icebox (junk) · Basic Fridge ·
Chill-Master (nice) · Cryo-Fridge (fancy, spoil ×2) · Frostbite Deluxe (luxury, + ice maker) · *Necro-Chef's Morgue
Locker* (signature: leftovers never spoil). **Upgrade in place**: a tinkering/handiness upgrade turns Basic → Nice
(cost = price difference × 0.8 + materials) so players keep their placement.

## 3. "Power" furniture (sought-after boost items)
A minority of items (~10 %) carry a **home aura**: a small, capped bonus while the item is in the home, e.g.
| Item (example) | Aura |
|---|---|
| Morgue Locker fridge | leftovers never spoil |
| Moonlit Mirror | Look Alive practice ×1.25 |
| Gramophone of the Damned | party mood +5 |
| Embalmer's Easel | painting quality +1 |
| Ancestor's Rocking Chair | Rest +10 % while seated |
Rules: auras are **capped** (max 3 active auras per home; same aura doesn't stack); every aura item has an
**earnable path** (quests, collections, contests, crafting) — store-only power items are forbidden (D-039 family);
power items are tagged and shown in the Collection Book; balance harness verifies households with auras stay within
BALANCE targets (no trivialising needs).

## 4. Contractors for additions & big builds
Small in-limit building stays instant (D-030). **Limit-raising projects** (rooms beyond cap, storeys, wings,
renovation levels — housing-progression spec) require a **contractor**:
- **Contractors are NPCs** who walk the neighbourhoods on schedules (deterministic per world), each with a
  **specialty** (Carpentry · Masonry · Plumbing · Electrical · Landscaping · Crypt-Digging for L6), a **crew size**
  (1–4 = how many projects at once) and a **quality rating** (speed/finish bonus).
- Find one (look for the hard hat icon on the street, at Rot Row Hardware, or the Bayou dock), **talk → hire**: they
  quote price + duration; if **booked**, they offer the next free slot. Friendship with a contractor gives discounts
  and priority.
- **Business cards**: after hiring a contractor once (or from quests/collections), you keep their card and can **call
  them by phone** without finding them again. A store **Contractor's Rolodex** (premium, convenience) adds a phone
  booking for any known contractor — never skips crew limits or timers (no paywall; D-035 spirit).
- Projects run on the build timer (housing spec) with the crew present on the lot (animations: hammer, saw, carry,
  dig — W-051 clips), noise moodlets for the household, and finish events.

## 5. Open, growing game
Furniture, contractors, foods, creatures and levels are **data + art packs** (content pack format, W-003/MONETIZATION
§5). Anyone with their own AI tokens can extend the game by building packs with the Creator Kit + MCP server (L7,
Phase 3) instead of burning the core team's budget — the core team keeps the engine, contracts and quality gates
(CI, clipping detector, balance harness) that every contributed pack must pass.
